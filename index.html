Who am I · Designer, maker, illustrator

I am drawn to the legend of the red thread: an invisible thread that joins people destined to meet, however far apart they are.

My pieces ask what separates us and what, in the end, connects us. I work with movement — discs that orbit, bands that spin — and with surface: what fire, pressure and time do to copper and brass.

I trained in 2D animation, worked as a graphic designer, illustrator and product designer, and founded two companies of my own. Returning to study jewellery was a deliberate choice: to master metal with my own hands.

    Drawing

    Ecoline · Watercolour · Pencil · Digital ink

    Drawing is how I design before going to the bench.

    I study how cabochons diffuse light while facets catch and fragment it. Before metal came twenty years of illustration: characters, portraits and animation.

    Live art

    Events · Weddings · Live fashion

    Live fashion illustration and portraits at weddings and events.

    I set up a small studio at the venue: ink, watercolour and markers, and a tablet with a quick reference photo. Each couple or guest is drawn on the spot — the outfit, the movement of the fabric, a gesture — then signed, dated and taken home the same day.

    The same skills I use at the bench: reading a silhouette fast, choosing what to leave out, and finishing a piece under pressure.

    Before the bench

    Since 1998

    From 1998 to 2002 I ran Hoang Chu SL, designing and producing fashion jewellery and accessories. From 2003 to 2011 I founded and ran Fushioko, importing decor, fashion and accessories from China, Thailand, Japan and Vietnam.

    Alongside, a small-scale practice: shrink-plastic pendants from my own illustrations (Nunca Beses Ranas) and hand-painted miniature ceramics.
